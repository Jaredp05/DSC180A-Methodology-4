**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?** 
- I would say the most interesting topic covered in the domain this quarter has been anomaly detection. While that is very general, that is what a majority of our quarter has consisted of, looking at data regarding electricity consumption in buildings and seeing what anomalies there are. My specific role has been working on making a dashboard, so an interesting part of anomaly detection has been finding what is relevant and what isn't. Our target audience is a building manager, so using our dashboard to highlight anomalies that may be significant in their case, and what is deemed 'significant' has been an interesting part of the quarter. \
